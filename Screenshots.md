Deployment. 
Create Stack > Under Prerequisite - Prepare Template > click Build from Infrastructure Composer > Create a Template in Infrastructure Composer > click Create in Infrastructure Composer > At the top there are two choices Canvas/Template > choose Template > then choose between and Yaml/Json > choose Yaml. Paste in yaml file from this github repo. Validate > create stack > look at the s3 bucket > confirm and continue to cloudformation > next > provide a stack name: "MyStack-ASG" > instance and ami are filled > add your subnets (at least 2) > select the VPCID, click and fill it (default) > next > next (look) > submit
